The plan

Three phases, each one useful on its own

Phase 1

Run the business and the pipeline

  • Back office: commissions, invoicing and payouts, without the manual entry
  • Lease schedules and billing that build themselves
  • Pipeline, forecasting and dashboards
  • Outlook connection
  • REN news alerts on brokers' contacts and companies
  • Data migration, testing and training
Phase 2

Multi-channel marketing

  • Email, website and listing campaigns from one place
  • Lead capture and routing
  • Document generation: marketing packages, pitch books and tour books
  • Campaign results brokers can see
Phase 3

Yardi integration and AI tools

  • Yardi sends over-the-term commissions to Salesforce each month
  • Salesforce sends money data to Yardi for the books
  • AI tools that log deals, contacts and follow-ups for brokers
