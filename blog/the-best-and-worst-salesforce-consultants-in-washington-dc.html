The Best and Worst Salesforce Consultants in Washington, DC

Salesforce is a powerful CRM that many businesses use to manage their sales and customer data. If you are looking for a Salesforce consultant in Washington, DC, it can be hard to know who to trust. There are many consultants out there, and there’s a huge variation in quality. Some are amazing, and some should probably stick to their day jobs! In this post, we’ll look at the best and worst Salesforce consultants in Washington, DC.

The good, the bad, and the ugly

First, why would someone hire a consultant for Salesforce? Many companies choose to outsource their IT needs instead of handling them in-house. Hiring a Salesforce consultant is a great way to get help from someone who knows the system inside and out and can help your business grow. It’s a robust program with tons of functionality. The genius of a good Salesforce implementation is in using only the features that actually fit the situation, and hiding everything else.

A good Salesforce consultant should:

  • Seek to understand your business and its unique processes
  • Help you set up and customize Salesforce to fit your needs
  • Train you and your team to use Salesforce effectively
  • Provide ongoing support and advice as needed

Now that we know what to look for in a good consultant, let’s look at the bad ones. A bad Salesforce consultant can do more harm than good, and end up costing your business money instead of saving it.

A bad Salesforce consultant may:

  • Not listen to your needs or understand what you want from the software
  • Take a long time to get back with answers and advice, leaving you waiting for months without any help at all. If this is happening, maybe they aren’t interested in helping you grow your business. Or they’re working on another project that takes priority, and you’re getting ignored.
  • Not give personalized service or advice, delivering the same generic “solutions” to everyone and every situation

You deserve better than that
